Data Deletion Request

If you would like Flowbyte Automations to delete all data associated with your Facebook or Instagram account, please follow the steps below.

To request deletion of your data:

  1. Send an email to [your contact email here] with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the Facebook Page name or Instagram username connected to our Service
  3. We will confirm your identity and process the deletion within 30 days

Once processed, all access tokens, automation configuration, and message/comment logs associated with your account will be permanently deleted from our systems.

Note: disconnecting your account (deauthorizing) does not automatically delete your data — a separate deletion request as described above is required for full data removal.
