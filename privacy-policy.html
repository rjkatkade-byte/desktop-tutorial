Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Flowbyte Automations ("we," "our," or "us") provides social media automation services (the "Service"), including automated replies to comments and direct messages on Facebook and Instagram, on behalf of businesses that connect their accounts to our platform ("Clients").

1. Information We Collect

When a Client connects their Facebook Page or Instagram Business account to Flowbyte Automations, we may access and store:

  • Basic profile information (name, username, profile picture)
  • Page/account access tokens required to send messages and manage comments on the Client's behalf
  • Comments and direct messages received on the connected account, solely for the purpose of generating automated responses
  • Configuration data the Client enters (automation rules, keywords, response templates)

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Operate the automation rules the Client has configured (e.g. auto-replying to comments or messages containing specific keywords)
  • Maintain logs of automation activity for the Client's own reporting and troubleshooting
  • Improve the reliability of the Service

We do not sell, rent, or share Client or end-user data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and configuration data are stored securely and are only accessible to authorized personnel operating the Service. We take reasonable technical measures to protect this data from unauthorized access.

4. Data Retention

We retain account access tokens and automation logs for as long as the Client's subscription is active. Upon cancellation or a data deletion request, this data is removed within a reasonable timeframe, as described in our Data Deletion Request process.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Our Service integrates with Meta's Graph API (Facebook and Instagram). Use of these platforms is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

Clients may revoke Flowbyte Automations' access to their Facebook Page or Instagram account at any time through their Meta account settings, or by contacting us directly. Clients may also request deletion of their data at any time.
